# Fingerprints

Every site you build with **scroll-craft** gets one row here, appended after it
ships. The registry exists so your next build can prove it is a different page
rather than a re-skin of one you already made.

This file is **yours**. It starts empty on purpose: the gate is about not
repeating *yourself*, so it has nothing to say until you have built something.

The rules and the gate live in the skill's
`references/uniqueness.md`. Short version:

**A new build must differ from EVERY row below on at least 4 of the 6
dimensions.** Four against each row individually, not four on average across the
table. If a planned build fails, change the plan. Never edit a row to make room
for it.

The six dimensions are: **grammar**, **nav treatment**, **hero device**,
**act-sequence shape**, **close pattern**, **signature move**.

Dimension 6 is free, because a signature move is unique by definition. So the
gate really asks for three more out of the remaining five, and a build that
changes only grammar and world will fail it.

## What is taken

Add a bullet here whenever a build claims something a later build should avoid
reusing: a grammar, a nav treatment, a close pattern, a signature move, an
act-count-and-length band. The shared columns are what the next build inherits
as a constraint, so writing them down is the whole point.

- **Split stage** with a document-space river divider whose bends are built from
  per-section splits, and a tile conveyor along it whose cargo is chosen by era
  (socialstream-split). Another split build needs a different divider device.
- **Death-notice hero** set against a live feed (socialstream-split).
- **Dwell-time interest reveal** carried into the form (socialstream-split).
- **Collapse close with a wordmark reunion** (socialstream-split).
- **HU/EN fixed switch as the only fixed chrome** is now shared by hb-social and
  socialstream-split; the next build should treat it as taken for nav credit.
- **Maison grammar**: an all-flow page on one ground with a film opener and a
  single night section (socialstream-maison).
- **Stream film hero**: a feed of vertical cards that stops and blooms one to
  full frame, with live HU/EN lines switched on the film's clock. Used three
  ways (Maison full-bleed, Journal as the cover photograph, Noir on black); the
  next build needs a different hero device.
- **Thumb stop**: media that plays only when the visitor stops scrolling on it
  (socialstream-maison).
- **Save tray carried into the form** (socialstream-journal). With the site's
  quiz result and the split's dwell reveal, a close that carries the visitor's
  own signal into the form is now taken three ways; the next close should not
  carry a result.
- **Fixed bar with wordmark, HU/EN and the audit** is worn by socialstream,
  socialstream-site and socialstream-maison, and the **dark form close** by the
  same three; neither earns nav or close credit any more.
- **Fixed contents rail with scroll spy** is shared by socialstream-cobalt and
  socialstream-journal.
- **Reel first**: a full-screen showreel with live text cues on the film's
  clock and a cursor play disc (socialstream-studio). By client request
  socialstream-edit, socialstream-feed and socialstream-exhibit open on the
  client's own brand film, full screen, so a full-screen film hero earns no
  hero credit any more.
- **Cursor-lit material**: a WebGL surface lit from the pointer behind the
  founder (socialstream-studio).
- **Cursor lens** over a blurred old-age feed (socialstream-studio).
- **Grid takeover** is now in hb-social, socialstream and socialstream-studio;
  use it again only if the client asks.
- **Cursor-glow bento** of services (socialstream-studio).
- **Editing-suite grammar**: one pinned suite where the scroll is the playhead
  and the timeline is the navigation (socialstream-edit), closed by an
  **export dialog** with a render bar.
- **Reels-app grammar**: snapped 9:16 posts with working actions, a comments
  sheet, stories and a poll (socialstream-feed), closed by a **DM** that asks
  one question at a time.
- **Gallery at night**: the cursor as the page's only light, with a light
  switch in the bar (socialstream-exhibit). With socialstream-studio's lit
  material and lens, a pointer-driven light is now taken twice.
- **Paper-object form close** (a tear-off reply card, an admission ticket) is
  shared by socialstream-journal and socialstream-exhibit.
- **The brand film's type system** (sans, italic Fraunces accent, serif figures on
  near-black with blue and cream fields) now dresses socialstream-edit and
  socialstream-feed rev 2; it is the client's look, not a device to reuse elsewhere.

## Appending a row

After shipping, add one line to the table and one bullet to **What is taken** if
the build claimed something new. Fill every column. Say what the build shares
with existing rows.

Rows are append-only. A build that has been superseded stays in the table,
because the space it occupies is still occupied.

## Worked example

The skill's author kept a registry of twelve builds across eight page grammars.
If you want to see what a filled-in table looks like, and which shapes tend to
collide, read `EXAMPLES.md` in the scroll-craft repository. Treat it as
illustration only: those rows are somebody else's builds and they do **not**
constrain yours.
