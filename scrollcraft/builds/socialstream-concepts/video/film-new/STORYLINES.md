# Brand film: three new storylines (2026-11-15)

The founder is not yet satisfied with film v5 ("Through the phone", `../film/`): too empty and dark, weak footage,
motion not premium, too long or slow. They asked for both a reworked v5 (`../film-v6/`) and a new idea, chosen from
three storylines in words. Nothing is built for a storyline before it is picked. Every line on screen is the film's
existing copy (Hungarian, and the English the founder approved on 2026-11-15); nothing new is claimed.

**Recommendation: A, because it acts out the claim itself (the feed dies, one interesting thing takes over) and keeps
every frame full of their own footage.**

## A. Nine to one (recommended)

Device: type carries the argument, on a grid of their work. Form: one frame that rebuilds.

The whole frame is a 3 x 3 grid of their footage, playing. The claim runs across it: the tiles stop and drain to grey
one by one as "social media" dies, and the one tile that keeps playing in colour grows until it is the whole frame.
The grid keeps rebuilding itself around their numbers and services, and never leaves the frame or goes dark.

- First two seconds: frame 0 is nine tiles of their work playing edge to edge (the FlyX cinema crowd, the arena
  stage, the Baltic sea, the beach crew, the Taurus mountains...) with thin night-blue gutters. At 0.4 s "A social
  media" lands across the middle row; at 1.4 s the first tile freezes and drains to grey.
- Beats: nine tiles alive → "meghalt.": the tiles freeze and turn grey one by one → one tile stays in colour and grows
  to the full frame: "Az érdeklődés vette át a helyét." → it splits back into tiles; three merge into one wide colour
  field for "81× ROAS", then "79 M Ft" → FlyX Tenerife takes four tiles → the six services, one tile each with its own
  footage → "300 M Ft" → "Mi ebben a korban győzünk." across the grid → the tiles fold into the S phone: the logo.
- Look: their footage in every tile; night blue only as 8 px gutters; the site's type (Sora, Instrument Serif) at
  full size; red-pink only on the living tile's edge and one full stop.
- Sound: a soft tick on each tile change, a low swell when the living tile takes over (the site plays it muted).
- Length: 18-20 s.

## B. From shoot to scroll

Device: a moment in the team's day, following one piece of content. Form: object-to-object chain.

We follow one video from the shoot to the people who stop for it: the crew on a beach, the camera's screen, the edit,
the phone, the crowd. Each shot ends on the thing that opens the next, in bright real places, never on a dark canvas.

- First two seconds: frame 0 is the crew running down the Baltic beach with a tripod in morning light, seen from
  behind, full frame. "A social media meghalt." lands in the sky above them; at 1.6 s a REC frame closes around the
  shot: we are in the camera's screen.
- Beats: the crew on the beach → the camera's screen (REC) → the shot drops onto an edit timeline under Bálint's hands
  → the finished reel slides into a phone → the phone's picture becomes the FlyX cinema, the crowd laughing: "Az
  érdeklődés vette át a helyét." → a colour flood carries the results ("81× ROAS", "79 M Ft") → the arena stage of
  Gyenis András, the services rolling past → "300 M Ft" → "Mi ebben a korban győzünk." → the logo.
- Look: daylight and real places; the REC frame, the timeline and the phone drawn thin in the site's white and blue;
  the site's type.
- Sound: a shutter click, a timeline scrub, a crowd laugh that swells (the site plays it muted).
- Length: 20-24 s.

## C. Interest in letters (the bold one)

Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat, the words are windows.

Giant words fill the frame edge to edge and their footage plays inside the letters, cutting on the beat. "SOCIAL
MEDIA" holds a grey, frozen feed; "ÉRDEKLŐDÉS" is full of their brightest work.

- First two seconds: frame 0 is "SOCIAL MEDIA" across the whole width, its letters a grey still of a feed on night
  blue. At 0.5 s a red-pink strike cuts through it; at 1.0 s the letters drop away and "meghalt." lands in serif
  italic.
- Beats: "SOCIAL MEDIA" (grey) → "meghalt." → "ÉRDEKLŐDÉS" fills the frame, the footage inside it cutting every half
  beat → the camera pushes into the Ő and its footage becomes the full frame → "81×", "79 M Ft", "300 M Ft" as giant
  numerals filled with the team's own sea footage (never a client's footage behind another client's number) → the
  services, one word per beat → "GYŐZÜNK." → its Ő turns into the logo's phone: the logo.
- Look: night blue ground; white type only where a word is not a window; red-pink only for the strike and the full
  stops.
- Sound: a 120 BPM beat that the cuts follow (the site plays it muted).
- Length: 14-16 s.

## Assumptions (all three)

Both cuts (wide 16:9, tall 9:16), in Hungarian and English, silent on the site; no voice, nothing paid. People whose
age or consent is unknown (the Türkiye youth exchange, Bridle Up Hope) stay small or seen from behind; results never
sit on a client's footage; the FlyX promo's ride-film shots (volcano, ship, pirate, cockpit) stay out, as they may be
the ride's licensed film rather than the team's footage.
