# Brand film v6: "Through the phone", reworked (spec, 2026-11-15)

The founder was not yet satisfied with v5 (`../film/`) and named all four problems offered to them: too empty and
dark, weak footage, motion not premium, too long or slow. They asked for both a reworked v5 (this folder) and a new
idea (`../film-new/STORYLINES.md`). v6 keeps v5's idea, one camera through the phone, and changes how every beat
looks.

```text
GOAL:    the same story as v5 (social media is dead, interest took its place; their work, their numbers, their
         services; "Mi ebben a korban győzünk."; the logo), told in about two thirds of the time with every frame
         full.
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, wide 1920 x 1080 and tall 1080 x 1920, 25 fps, silent (the site plays it muted and looping),
         Hungarian and English. Draft first: wide Hungarian only, until the founder approves it.
TIME:    22.52 s x 25 = 563 frames. The last frame equals frame 0 (the loop has no jump).
LOOK:    every frame is full: their footage edge to edge, or a full colour field (the site's blue, violet and
         red-pink turned up, with slow lights). Ink white; on the sunset, ink night #05070f. Display Sora 700
         (tracking -4.5 %), accent word Instrument Serif italic, small type Inter 700 at 28 px or more. One red-pink
         accent per frame (the strike, the ×, a full stop, the eyebrow's dot).
MOTION:  swipes power3.inOut 0.38 s; the dive power2.in on a log scale; the back-out power3.inOut 0.8 s. Type lands
         by focus and opacity (words 67-80 ms apart, letters of a name 0.1 s apart, numbers with a 5 % scale
         settle) and is dead still until its slide leaves. Render-time motion blur with a short shutter (100°).
BANNED:  a dark void with small cards floating in it; odometers and smeared numbers; titles that drift after
         landing; the same face in more than one beat; a result over a client's footage; the FlyX promo's
         ride-film shots (aerials, volcano, ship, pirate, cockpit); stock B-roll; children in the foreground.
```
