# SocialStream: three templates (Orbit, Screen, Bento)

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. What is quoted is the client's;
the rest is authored.

## What the client said (evidence)

After Stream and six colour versions of it:

- "What you're bringing me is different colors, but I need complete different
  build options. Because, for example, I don't really like the side thingies that
  are goes to the side and are there in some places. Also, I want some of the
  features to come back that I said. For example, on the mastermind page, I like
  how there are clear thingies, design elements in the background ... This is why
  I want three different templates and builds and things, not just bunch of
  different colors."
- While they were being built: "It doesn't have to stick to this color palette.
  If you suggest any picture change, for example, there is the door handle and
  all of those static pictures that has nothing to do with the new page. Those
  can be changed, so you can look for better pictures or generate better pictures
  ... And also, after we have the good design, then we will re-edit the video for
  the hero section."
- Before that, on colour: "i like dusk the best"; "reviera not bad but i dont
  like the too bright colors".
- The references they sent earlier, which "the features I said" points back to:
  mastermind.com (clear shapes behind the product, concentric outlines, hand-drawn
  arrows round a callout, one giant counted number), capmarketing.hu (a phone
  whose screen changes beside passing captions, text that lights up), a TikTok of
  an agency site (tall cards rising over a pinned statement, the next line
  landing on top), helloagency.hu (one drawing per service).
- Still in force: their brand film opens every page full screen with nothing over
  it (it will be re-cut once a design is chosen); only real numbers; testimonials
  verbatim; Hungarian first with English; no em dashes; no video beyond the film.

## What that asks for

1. Three builds that differ in structure, not in colour.
2. Nothing drifting at the page edges. A background element is placed, large and
   clear, and stands behind a thing (a phone, a card, a form), never behind text.
3. The references' own devices, used properly this time.
4. Free colour. Each template has its own palette so a choice between them is
   not skewed by sameness, and any palette fits any template.
5. The four still lifes go. See Pictures.

## The three templates

| | Orbit | Screen | Bento |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grammar | Centred showcase: every section is one thing in the middle with a few large shapes behind it | One device, passing captions: a phone holds its place and its screen answers each caption | Tiles: the page is a board, and what is not a tile is a card |
| Reference it answers | mastermind.com | capmarketing.hu | the TikTok (cards rise over a statement), bento layouts |
| Signature move | The phone rises as a product shot among a sphere, a hoop and a pill; handwritten notes point at it; later one number is counted across the whole screen | Six captions, four screens: the grey feed of who you follow, the feed of what interests you, a profile with 10 000 followers and 40 likes, a reel watched to the end, the old rules and the new | The first sentence stands alone; eight tiles rise over it one by one and the second sentence lands last on the largest; then three law cards stack |
| The laws | giant count pin, a ring that fills, struck-out rules | all three inside the phone | three stacking cards (a 10 000-cell grid, the ring, the rules) |
| Work | a fan of seven reels on a ribbon | a profile grid | a mosaic |
| Services | a card each, with its scene | a list with one panel | drawers |
| Results | four cards, a drawn object in each corner | four stories | a ledger |
| Voices | one at a time, large | comments | a wall of cards |
| Background | the most: 3D shapes, rings, a ribbon, notes | shapes only behind the phone; soft light elsewhere | the least: soft light and outlines; shapes live inside tiles |
| Palette | Dusk (violet, rose, amber) | Lagoon (petrol, teal, sand, coral) | Ember (coal, copper, amber) |

The amount of background decoration is graded on purpose (most, some, least),
because "clear things in the background" and "no side things" pull against each
other and the client's answer will say where the line is.

## Pictures

- The four case still lifes (door handle, bracket, watch, pen) are gone. In their
  place are four objects drawn in code from the same shapes and colours as the
  page: an open door, a hall, a wristwatch, a screen of registrants with a health
  cross. They recolour with the palette and cannot be mistaken for a client's
  photograph. Generated images were not used: an object that belongs to the
  page's own shape language was the better fit, and it costs the client nothing.
- Real pictures stay real: fourteen frames from their client reels, the three
  portraits, the founder.
- The hero is still the current brand film; it is a placeholder for the re-cut.

## Feeling curve (each template)

Attention (film) > the claim, the page's loudest moment > the three laws
(recognition, hope, resolve) > the work (pleasure in looking) > services
(substance) > results and voices (proof) > people (trust) > the audit
(commitment) > the name. The peak is the claim in Orbit and Bento and Law I in
Screen (the count inside the phone).

## Not done, on purpose

- No stream of cards and nothing at the page edges.
- No fabricated counts in the phone's interface: only 10 000, 40 and 0.4%.
- The shared blocks (services, results, voices, people, the form) are written by
  one script so the three stay in step while a choice is made. The chosen
  template gets them written into its HTML.
