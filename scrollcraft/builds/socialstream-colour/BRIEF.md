# SocialStream: Stream, in colour

A second round on [socialstream-stream](../socialstream-stream/BRIEF.md), which
the client liked and asked to keep as it is. That brief holds the subject, the
journey, the grammar ("Plain page, one living figure"), the peak and the
tell-someone sentence; all of them stand here. This file records what the client
said next and what was decided because of it. Self-authored under explicit
creative delegation; what is not quoted is authored.

## What the client said (evidence)

- "Give me two or three other designs. One of my problems is it's too basic
  colors, and it's not too colorful. It doesn't have to be like neon like in
  some of the other those, but it feels like too minimalistic, let's say. But
  actually I like this design, so let's leave this one but make two or three
  other new versions."
- While it was being built: "also not enough pictures, need better motion
  graphycs. needs to feel 2026".
- Still in force from earlier rounds: their own 30 s brand film opens the page,
  full screen, nothing over it; only real numbers; testimonials verbatim;
  Hungarian first with an English switch; no video beyond the brand film.
  ("We dont need to over feed it with all of these reels and videos" was said
  of playing clips. Stills are a different thing, and they now ask for more.)

## What that asks for

1. **Colour, not neon.** Three colour worlds of the same page, each with five or
   six fields instead of three, so the page changes ground as it goes.
2. **More pictures.** Their own, since they have them: frames from the client
   reels they made (captions and all, so they read as posts), the four
   portraits, the four case still lifes.
3. **Better motion graphics.** The stream's cards were plain rectangles; the
   abstraction was the weak point. Here a card is a post.
4. **2026.** Sections laid on each other as sheets with round corners, headlines
   that arrive word by word, a phone that does what the copy says, pictures
   that pop in at full strength instead of fading.

## The three looks

| Look | Ground | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Riviera** | navy, sea blue, coral, sun yellow, sand | Flat blocks, the accent word on a tilted block of colour, a sand edge round every picture. Tenerife is where they are. |
| **Dusk** | near-black and plum with rose, amber, violet and teal light; cream for the light sections | Soft light in the ground, the accent word in a warm gradient, hairline edges. |
| **Paper** | paper white with butter, pink, sky and mint; ink and cobalt for contrast | A dotted ground, an ink outline and a hard shadow on every card, a wavy rule under the accent word. |

One page, one script; a look is a block of custom properties (see the README).
The type is the brand film's in all three.

## What changed against Stream

- **The stream carries pictures.** One atlas of 32 pictures (24 reel frames,
  four portraits, four still lifes). The nearer background cards are posts, the
  small ones take the field's colours. The forty that light up in Law I are
  posts. Law III is two hundred posts thrown about, then set in rows.
  A post is never under the text: it keeps to the page's own margin.
- **The name, explained, gets a picture.** A phone beside the copy leaves the
  "Követés" (Following) tab for "Neked" (For You); then post after post, with
  one tag at a time beside it. It says the page's claim in one image.
- **The work.** A new section, two running rows of 24 frames, under their own
  phrase from the team copy: "Ami megállítja a hüvelykujjat."
- **Services.** The six line drawings became six small scenes in a panel: two of
  their posts with one playing, nine posts falling into a grid, a line climbing
  to 81× ROAS, a page building itself round the watch, three steps, a funnel.
- **Results.** The client's still life is stuck down beside each number.
- **Voices** are cards in the look's swatches; **people** stand on blocks of
  colour; a **ticker** of the five real numbers runs once, after the phone.

## Feeling curve (acts that are new or changed)

The curve is Stream's. Two acts were added and they are both calm ones, so the
peak (Law I) keeps the largest pinned span and still follows a quiet act:

- After the claim: the phone (curiosity, a small "oh, that is the point").
- After Law III: the work rows (pleasure in looking; no claim, no number).

## Not done, on purpose

- No new fingerprint claim: the grammar, the chrome and the signature move are
  Stream's. This is a restyle and an enrichment of it, logged as such.
- No client is named beside a picture. The frames show what they show; the copy
  says only "frames from the videos we made for our clients".
- No frame with a child in the foreground, no guest-review frame, no end card
  with a client's prices, no stock B-roll from the client reels.
