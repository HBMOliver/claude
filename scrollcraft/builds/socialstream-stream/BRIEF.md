# SocialStream: Stream

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The client set the direction in
their own words and chose the idea; the decisions below that are not quoted are
authored.

## What the client said (evidence)

- "I actually like the feed better, but ... it's too complicated and people are
  going to be ... not happy about it. I want something that's simple. It's a
  normal website, but it's unique and it gives back the feeling of social
  stream. ... Give me some ideas and please work on your best scrolling
  animations. If it's needed, make motion graphics to the website."
- Of three ideas offered, they chose "The Stream": a stream of small vertical
  posts behind an ordinary page that forms pictures of their real numbers.
- Sites they like: thedeepcc.com (bold type, hard colour blocks, numbered
  services), followmarketing.eu (plain and direct), capmarketing.hu (a phone
  with vertical video, text that lights up on scroll), helloagency.hu ("the more
  unique one": flat fields, one drawing per service).
- "animations here": a TikTok of a luxury agency site where tall portrait cards
  rise over a centred statement and a new line lands on top of them.
- mastermind.com: "i love how they have things in the background".
- Standing rules from earlier rounds: their own 30 s brand film opens the page,
  full screen, nothing over it; the film's own look (near-black, warm white,
  dusty blue, steel-blue and cream fields; Plus Jakarta Sans with an italic
  Fraunces accent and Fraunces figures); only real numbers; testimonials
  verbatim; Hungarian first with an English switch; no footage beyond the film.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Simple, normal, bold, alive, theirs. (Authored from the references:
   plain structure, very large statements, flat fields, one device.)
2. **Journey.** Film, the claim, three laws, services, results, client voices,
   the people, the audit. Their content order, unchanged.
3. **Energy.** Loud at the film and at Law I. Calm through services, voices and
   people. Firm and still at the end.
4. **Feeling and the one moment.** See the curve and the peak below.
5. **The one thing no site does.** One stream of exactly 10 000 cards runs the
   whole page: it is the film's own picture at the top, every number's picture
   in the middle and the name at the end.
6. **Range.** Premium-minimal moved toward bold and plain: the film's palette,
   big sans statements, hard cuts between fields.
7. **One world or scenes.** Distinct sections on hard-cut fields, joined by one
   stream. Not a worldflight.
8. **Assets.** The brand film (16:9 and 9:16), four studio portraits, four case
   still lifes. Nothing generated for this build.

## Subject, belief, action

- **What it is.** SocialStream, an interest media agency in Tenerife and
  Budapest, for brands with stable revenue that treat marketing as an investment.
- **What the visitor must believe.** Social media has become interest media, and
  this agency knows the new rules and has the numbers.
- **The one action.** Ingyenes audit / Free audit, the same label everywhere.

## Grammar

**Plain page, one living figure** (named new). A section is an ordinary website
section; where it makes a claim with a number, the section is the caption and
the stream is the figure. Chrome is an ordinary site menu. The ending is the
figure resolving into the name above an ordinary form.

Bans: no second picture system for arguments (photographs are for people and the
still lifes only), no scrubbed video acts, no pan rails, carousels or snapping,
no app chrome, nothing that works only with a pointer. Every picture is made of
the same 10 000 cards, each count is literal and each figure states its unit.

## Signature move

The stream. Ten thousand vertical cards in one layer between the section
grounds and the text. At the top they are the film (each card a tile of the
playing frame); the film breaks into a wall of small screens and pours down the
page. They then draw each claim in units: 10 000 followers of which 40 light up;
a crowd gathering round one post; scattered cards falling into one current;
1 becoming 81; 79; 23; about 5 700 people. They drift behind the quiet sections
and come together as the name at the end. They move with the scroll and part
round the cursor.

## Feeling curve

```
1   Attention     their own film, full screen, nothing over it
2   Surprise      under the visitor's hand the film breaks into ten thousand small screens
3   Clarity       "A social media meghalt." covered by rising posts; the second sentence lands on top
4   Recognition   the name explained in one plain line, the stream running beside it
5   Sting (peak)  10 000 cards assemble while the count runs, then all but 40 go dark
6   Hope          on blue, a crowd gathers round one post as it is watched to the end
7   Resolve       on cream, scattered cards fall into one current; the old rules are struck
8   Calm          six services in large plain type; the stream at the edges only
9   Confidence    each real number counted and drawn in units
10  Warmth        client voices in their own words; almost nothing moves (authored quiet)
11  Trust         faces: the founder, then the three
12  Decision      a plain form on cream, the capacity stated
13  Resolution    the stream comes together as the name, and holds
```

## The peak

Act 5. "Ten thousand little cards filled the screen while the number counted up,
and then all but forty went dark." It has the largest pinned span on the page;
the act before it is a single quiet line.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where their film breaks into ten thousand tiny posts that flow
down the page with you, and then shows you what 40 likes out of 10 000 followers
actually looks like.

## Authored silence

Acts 10 and 11 (voices, people): the stream thins to a few cards at the page
edges and nothing else moves. This is intended; it is not dead scroll.

## Score

| Beat | Device | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | short pin, the film untouched | the client's rule; the page starts on their own work |
| Turn | bespoke: film tiles to stream; pinned layered statement with rising cards | the reference they pointed at, and it tells the claim: the old sentence is covered, the new one lands on top |
| Recognition | flow, words lit by scroll | one plain line after a loud act |
| Sting | pin, scrubbed count, the grid | the number has to be seen whole, so the frame holds |
| Hope | flow with a held figure, watch bar | a calmer cousin of the peak, on a new field |
| Resolve | flow, strike and reveal | a change of state |
| Substance | flow, hover and scroll-active list | plain reading; the reference sites all do this plainly |
| Proof | flow, scrubbed counts with unit figures | real figures only |
| Warmth, trust | flow with reveals | quiet |
| Commitment | flow, the form | an ordinary form is the right ending for an ordinary page |
| Resolution | the stream forms the wordmark | the figure that ran the page becomes the name |

Families: pin, flow with reveals, kinetic (scroll-lit words), count, reveal
(strikes, line masks), pointer. The run of flow sections after Law III is the
shape of an ordinary website and each one uses a different inner device.
