# BRIEF: HB Marketing, /social

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The request was "using all
the installed skills and added features, help make hbmarketing.hu/social a luxury
social media [page]". No interview answers exist; everything below is an authored
decision unless marked as evidence.

## Evidence vs assumption

- Evidence: the brand is HB Marketing, domain hbmarketing.hu, the page lives at `/social`.
- Evidence: the live page could not be read. hbmarketing.hu does not resolve from
  this environment (DNS failure, proxy refusal), so nothing of the current copy,
  pricing or client list is carried over.
- Assumption: a Budapest agency selling social media management to premium
  Hungarian and international brands (hospitality, fashion, jewellery, real estate,
  private clinics). Hungarian is the primary language, English the second.
- Assumption: no real client names, results or prices are available, so the page
  states none. No counters, no testimonials, no logos.
- Constraint: no usable photography. Higgsfield had 0 credits; Canva generated
  eight on-brief stills but full-size downloads are blocked by the network policy.
  Per uniqueness.md §2.5 this is the case where type should carry the page.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe:** hushed, tailored, unhurried, a little theatrical. References: a
   couture house's autumn invitation card, the oxblood salon in *Phantom Thread*,
   a Bodoni title page.
2. **Journey:** one word at monumental scale; a quiet honest paragraph; the
   conviction in a single line; the feed assembling itself; the atelier's
   services; how working together goes; a small private invitation.
3. **Energy:** loud open, quiet second, loud third, the loudest at the peak,
   then steadily calmer to a whisper at the close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** see curve. The moment: nine scattered posts
   flying together into one picture.
5. **What no other site does:** the page shows a grid takeover happening to it.
6. **Range:** premium-minimal, explicitly asked for ("luxury"), tilted editorial.
7. **One world or scenes:** distinct scenes, hard grounds. No continuous world.
8. **Assets:** none usable. Fully typographic, with a procedurally rendered
   satin plate (SVG lighting, rendered once to WebP in headless Chromium).

## Grammar

Typographic poster (§2.5). Filmic lost (no footage, and it is the drift default);
chaptered editorial would bury the one claim; live surface needs a product;
continuous world needs geography; gallery needs photographs of objects; split
stage has no two sides; cutlist is the wrong energy for luxury.

## Feeling curve

1. **Hush**: one word, Atelier, at 30vw on oxblood satin, receding as you scroll.
2. **Recognition**: two small paragraphs naming the hurried feed, after the volume.
3. **Conviction**: "Fewer posts. Better ones." wiped across the letterforms, huge.
4. **Delight (peak)**: nine scattered tiles fly together into one composed picture.
5. **Appetite**: the atelier index, each service opening on request.
6. **Trust**: the method, an actual sequence, set calmly on bone.
7. **Resolve**: the smallest type on the site, one underlined line to write to us.

**The peak:** "the feed literally built itself into one picture while I scrolled."
Act 4, largest span on the page (3.2vh), preceded by a flow act that holds still.

**Tell-someone sentence:** It's the site where the Instagram grid assembles
itself into a single picture while you scroll.

**Authored silence:** the gap between act 2's paragraphs and act 3 is deliberate
breathing room on the same ground, not dead scroll.

## Signature move

The grid takeover: nine tiles scattered in depth across the viewport, each
holding one ninth of a single composition, converge under scroll into a 3x3
feed and lock into one picture. Bespoke JS reading the act's `--sc-p`.
