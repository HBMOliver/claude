# BRIEF: SocialStream Noir (the split stage, restyled)

**A restyle by client request, not a new build.** The client found the
split-stage flagship (`socialstream-split`) interesting but not luxury, and
asked for one design that keeps the split concept in a high-end register,
alongside two that elevate the cream site (`socialstream-maison`,
`socialstream-journal`). Everything structural is the split build's: grammar,
the river marker, the death-notice hero, the act sequence, the collapse close,
the interest reveal, every line of copy. Its BRIEF carries the journey, feeling
curve and score; this file records only what changed and why.

## What changed

| | Split (flagship) | Noir |
|---|---|---|
| Dead age | Grey paper, EB Garamond | Ivory paper (#ECE6DC), Fraunces at light weights |
| Living age | Petrol ground, amber, Mona Sans | Warm black (#100F0D), pale blue (#A9C0D3), Fraunces + Plus Jakarta Sans |
| The divider | A river of tiles: a conveyor whose cargo changes by era | The bare edge between the two grounds, still the same D(y), still bending left section by section |
| Phones | Horizontal stream bands between the stacked blocks | No bands; the blocks meet edge to edge |
| Hero reel | The 41-cut graded sizzle | The stream film rendered on the black ground (`film-ink-*`) |
| Death notice | Heavy 4 px black border and ribbon on grey card | Hairline double rule and a slimmer ribbon on an ivory card |
| Buttons | Amber | Cream pills |
| Peak cards | Fly out of the tile stream at the divider | The same flight, from the bare divider |

**Why**: luxury is mostly subtraction. The tile conveyor was the loudest thing
on the page, so it went; the two grounds were pulled to the brand's own cream
and a warm black; the display type became the brand's own serif on both sides,
so the two ages differ by ground and by voice, not by font.
