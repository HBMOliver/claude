# SocialStream: cobalt edition

A second, completely different design of the full SocialStream site. Same content
and features as `socialstream-site/`; new visual system and structure.

- Palette: one cobalt (#1f3fff), cool paper, true ink. All photography black and
  white so cobalt is the only colour.
- Type: Archivo (condensed 62% width, uppercase display), Geist (text), Geist Mono (data).
- Hero: departures-board flip, SOCIAL to INTEREST, over a B&W cutout of Olivér
  (laptop portrait, rembg) on the cobalt field. Click the board or "Újra" to replay.
- Structure: fixed chapter rail 00-07 with scroll spy (desktop), full-screen cobalt
  index (mobile); laws as a sideways pan through ink, cobalt and paper panels;
  services as a numbered board; results as a wall of giant numbers with hover
  previews and case dialogs; one-at-a-time testimonials; footer-wide wordmark.

Same launch notes as the other build: the form hands off to mailto; connect a real
form backend for production. David and Lidi load from the live site's CDN.
