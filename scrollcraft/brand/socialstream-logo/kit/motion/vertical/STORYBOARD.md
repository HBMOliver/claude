# SocialStream logo animation, vertical: storyboard (as built)

The horizontal's story and clock (`../horizontal/STORYBOARD.md`) on the stacked lockup: the phone does its four
beats in the middle of the frame, rises into its place above the name, then SOCIAL drops out from under the
phone and STREAM from under SOCIAL, so the name still comes out of the phone, in reading order. The last frame
is the kit's stacked dark lockup (`../../source/socialstream-logo-stacked-dark.svg`) at 60 % of the frame
width.

1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 4.6 s, silent; for Reels, TikTok and Stories. Night ground, Cream phone and SOCIAL, Stream
Blue STREAM (the variables `night`, `cream` and `stream` in `index.html`).

| Time (s) | Beat | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00–1.37 | As the horizontal: the phone arrives upright and empty (38 % of the frame height, 3.2× its lockup size), the S swipes up into it, it leans in 15°, the 15° cut opens | same eases and times |
| 1.45–2.10 | Into the lockup | 3.2× → 1×, rising to its place above the name (power3.inOut, 0.65 s) |
| 1.90–2.60 | SOCIAL drops out from under the phone | slides down from behind a level edge at the phone's lowest point (expo.out, 0.7 s) |
| 2.02–2.72 | STREAM drops out from under SOCIAL | slides down from behind a level edge just below SOCIAL's baseline, which moves with SOCIAL (expo.out, 0.7 s); blur 8 → 0 px on the name from 1.90 (0.5 s) |
| 2.32–2.52 | The A's cut opens like the phone's | expo.out, 0.2 s |
| 2.6–4.60 | Hold | dead still; the last frame is the kit master |

Generated by `../../../tools/make_motion.py vertical`, from the same geometry as the horizontal.
