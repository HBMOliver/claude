# SocialStream logo animation, horizontal: storyboard (as built)

One idea, told by the logo's own construction: a phone appears, the next reel swipes up into it, it leans in
at 15°, a 15° cut takes its foot off, and the name streams out from behind it. The A's cut echoes the phone's.
The last frame is the kit's dark horizontal lockup (`../../source/socialstream-logo-horizontal-dark.svg`, the
reversed drawing) at 60 % of the frame width.

1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 4.6 s, silent. Night #05070F ground, Cream #F1EEE6 phone and SOCIAL, Stream Blue
#7FA1C4 STREAM (the variables `night`, `cream` and `stream` in `index.html`).

| Time (s) | Beat | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00–0.50 | The phone arrives, upright and empty, at 3× its lockup size, centre frame | scale 0.9 → 1 and opacity 0 → 1 (expo.out, 0.5 s); blur 14 → 0 px (0.45 s) |
| 0.25–0.75 | The next reel swipes up into the screen: the S | slides up from below the screen, clipped to it (expo.out, 0.5 s) |
| 0.75–1.30 | It leans in | 0 → 15° about its centre with about 1° of overshoot (back.out(1.4), 0.55 s) |
| 1.15–1.37 | The cut | the 15° slice opens from a hairline to its full width (expo.out, 0.22 s) |
| 1.45–2.10 | Into the lockup | 3× → 1× and to its place on the left (power3.inOut, 0.65 s) |
| 1.90–2.60 | The name streams out from behind the phone | a wipe whose edge is parallel to the phone's side runs left to right (power3.out, 0.65 s); SOCIAL, then STREAM 0.12 s later, slide the last 40 px into place (expo.out, 0.7 s); blur 8 → 0 px (0.5 s) |
| 2.32–2.52 | The A's cut opens like the phone's | expo.out, 0.2 s, once the name is sharp |
| 2.52–4.60 | Hold | dead still; the last frame is the kit master |

## Build

Generated by `../../../tools/make_motion.py` from the kit's geometry (`kitmark.py`, `build_kit.py`, `caps.py`):
the phone is drawn untilted and the lean adds the 15°, which reproduces the kit symbol exactly (the script
asserts it). Every animated transform is an explicit SVG transform string that GSAP interpolates, and both
cuts open by shrinking clip rectangles over static fill shapes that follow the outline exactly, so nothing
crosses the phone's or the A's edge while they open. Two layers: the name under the phone, so it can come out
from behind it.
