# SocialStream logo animation, square: storyboard (as built)

The vertical version (`../vertical/STORYBOARD.md`) in a square frame: the same beats, clock and stacked lockup.
The phone does its four beats in the middle of the frame, rises into its place above the name, then SOCIAL drops
out from under the phone and STREAM from under SOCIAL. The last frame is the kit's stacked dark lockup
(`../../source/socialstream-logo-stacked-dark.svg`) at 60 % of the frame width.

1080 × 1080, 30 fps, 4.6 s, silent; for feed posts. Colours as variables (`night`, `cream`, `stream`).

| Time (s) | Beat | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00–1.37 | The phone arrives upright and empty (45 % of the frame height, 2.1× its lockup size), the S swipes up into it, it leans in 15°, the 15° cut opens | as the horizontal |
| 1.45–2.10 | Into the lockup | 2.1× → 1×, rising to its place above the name (power3.inOut, 0.65 s) |
| 1.90–2.72 | SOCIAL drops out from under the phone, then STREAM from under SOCIAL | as the vertical |
| 2.32–2.52 | The A's cut opens like the phone's | expo.out, 0.2 s |
| 2.6–4.60 | Hold | dead still; the last frame is the kit master |
