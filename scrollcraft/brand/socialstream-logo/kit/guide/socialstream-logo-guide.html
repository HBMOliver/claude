Logo guide

A bold S on a 9:16 phone that leans in at 15°; its foot is sliced off at the same 15° as the A in SOCIAL. Every cut and lean in the logo is 15°. Always use the files; never rebuild the logo.

Versions

Horizontal is the default; stacked for square formats and shirts; the phone alone where the name is already there (profile pictures, favicon, the corner of a reel). Light versions (Night + Deep Stream) go on cream or white, dark versions (Cream + Stream Blue) on night blue or calm, dark photos.

Clear space

Keep at least x clear on every side: x is the cap height of SOCIAL. For the phone alone, x is half the phone's width.

Typefaces

Montserrat for headlines: ExtraBold caps for statements, Light for the second voice. Plus Jakarta Sans for body text. Both are free (SIL Open Font License). The logo's letters are custom outlines based on Montserrat (the sliced A, S terminals cut at 75°, an M at exact 60°), so the logo can't be typed.
