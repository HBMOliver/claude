What we heard

The brief

An interest media agency (Tenerife and Budapest) for established businesses: short-form video, paid ads, websites, automation and growth. The claim: social media is dead, interest has taken its place. The mark should be confident and modern, work as a profile picture and on reels, and grow out of the team's own shirt wordmark.

confidentmodernalivedirect

Success: reads at 16 px and as a round profile picture, works white on video, one colour, on night blue and on cream; clearly not a copy of any agency's mark.

Final design

G2 · The phone that leans in

A bold S on a 9:16 phone that leans in at 15 degrees; its foot is sliced off at the same 15 degrees as the A in SOCIAL.

  • Grows out of the team's own shirt wordmark: SOCIAL heavy, STREAM light, the A sliced once
  • One rule ties it together: every cut and lean is 15 degrees (the A, the phone's slice, its tilt, the S terminals)
  • The phone is the reel: it works as the profile picture, the favicon and the corner mark on video
