# SocialStream logo: brief and research (round 1)

Written from what we already know about the agency, without a questionnaire. The assumptions are listed at
the end; correct any of them and the concepts move with it.

## Design brief

**Summary.** SocialStream is an interest media agency (Tenerife and Budapest, a team of three) that makes
short-form video, runs paid ads on Meta, TikTok and Google, builds websites and landing pages, and sets up
automation, email and growth strategy for established businesses that treat marketing as an investment.

**Claim.** "A social media meghalt. Az érdeklődés vette át a helyét." Feeds now run on interest, not on
followers.

**Audience.** Owners and marketing leads of webshops, B2B firms, webinar and event organisers and local
services. They meet the mark as a profile picture, as the end card and corner mark on reels, on the website
header, in proposals and decks, and on the brand film's title card.

**Brand adjectives.** Restrained, editorial, alive, confident, warm.
**Avoid feeling.** Salesy, neon, template or stock, "growth-hacker".

**Promise / big idea.** Interest takes the place of social: the italic (how type shows emphasis and
movement) takes the place of the upright.

**Competitive landscape.** Social and marketing marks lean on blue, containers and speech bubbles (in the
reference library's social-communication set: blue 39 %, containment 41 %, speech bubbles 25 %), plus
play buttons, hearts, wifi waves, phones and upward arrows. Open territory: an editorial serif with one
quiet accent, and symbols about attention rather than broadcasting.

**Mark type hypothesis.** Wordmark-led (the name is the asset and the wordmark already has equity), with a
compact companion mark for avatars, favicons and reel corners. Explore a letterform, an abstract symbol
and a combination mark next to it.

**Must work on.** Night blue / near-black and near-white / cream grounds; 16 px favicon; circular avatar
crop; white or cream over video; one colour; motion (reel end cards, website cursor effects).

**Constraints.** Keep the equity of the current wordmark (Fraunces roman "Social" + Fraunces italic
"Stream" in dusty blue). Palette from the brand film: near-black, warm white, dusty blue #7fa1c4, steel
blue #7697b4, cream #f1eee6. No concentric rings, no neon, nothing stock-looking. No invented claims.

**Success criteria.** Reads at 16 px and as a small corner mark on video; works white on video; stands
apart from platform glyphs; feels premium editorial, not salesy; explainable in one sentence.

**Decision-maker.** The founder. **Deliverables this round:** four concepts as SVG, tests, one overview
sheet. The full kit only after a direction is chosen.

## The current wordmark (equity audit)

Measured against the brand film's title card (`scrollcraft/builds/socialstream-concepts/assets/hero-poster.jpg`):
Fraunces at optical size 144 and weight 400, roman "Social" in warm white, italic "Stream" in dusty blue,
set tight with no space, "INTEREST MEDIA ÜGYNÖKSÉG" letterspaced in Plus Jakarta Sans underneath.

- Keep: Fraunces roman + italic, the colour split, the tight join at l|S.
- Problem: it is unmodified Fraunces, so anyone can type it, and it has no small-size companion.

## Clichés kept out

Play-button triangles, speech bubbles, hearts and likes, wifi or broadcast waves, water waves or a river S
for "stream", phone outlines, megaphones, upward arrows and charts, a plain S in a stock font. And, for
"pull" or "attention": targets, ripples, orbits, spirals (relatives of the disliked rings), magnets, eyes,
funnels, sparkles, an S drawn as a ribbon.

**Cultural check.** Two matching, parallel or angular S's can read as the SS insignia to a Hungarian and
European audience. None of the concepts places two S's side by side; the existing wordmark's roman S and
italic S are clearly unequal, and concept C merges them into one.

## Word map (abridged)

- **social** → profile circle, followers, crowd, upright, roman, the past tense of the claim
- **stream** → feed, scroll, sequence, reels, 9:16 frames, motion, italic
- **interest** → lean in, emphasis (italic), point of interest, the one that stands out, the cursor
- **media / craft** → the cut, the frame, the hook, the loop

Intersections: the italic taking the upright's place; a frame something passes through; one letter that is
both words; a feed in which one item leans out.

## Round 2 inputs (from the founder)

- The team shirt carries an earlier wordmark: SOCIAL in heavy Montserrat caps (the A's crossbar replaced by a
  slanted slice), STREAM in a light weight. It is one shirt, so it can be improved freely.
- They like round 1's B (the S on a reel, read as a phone).
- The shirt was imagined as "a kind of Social Chain logo". Social Chain's current logotype is a heavy
  grotesk "SocialChain" whose a in "Social" is an italic serif a; take its confidence, not that device.

## Assumptions

- The name stays "SocialStream", one word, two capitals.
- The roman/italic split and the dusty blue are the equity worth keeping.
- Most use is digital (social, web, video); print is secondary (cards, decks).
- Hungarian is the main market; English must work too.
- Fraunces and Plus Jakarta Sans stay the brand typefaces (both SIL Open Font License, which allows logo use).
