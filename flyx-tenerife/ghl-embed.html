How FlyX works

Thirty minutes, beautifully simple.

30:00min
Arrive

Take your seat

Find us at The Duke Shops in Costa Adeje. Flights leave every 30 minutes, from 10:00 to 22:00, every day.

10 min · The story

Tenerife has its secrets

Your experience starts with a 10-minute film exploring Tenerife’s history, geology and local stories: facts you’ll catch yourself repeating later.

15 min · The flight

The excitement begins

Then you move on to the immersive flight. Gently moving seats carry you over cliffs, coastlines, valleys, forests and volcanic landscapes.

Why FlyX is different?

The smart way to see Tenerife

Tenerife is stunning, but exploring it isn’t always easy.

Long drives. Winding mountain roads. Heat, crowds, parking, tired kids.

FlyX offers a completely different way to experience the island.

  • No cars
  • No weather worries
  • No physical effort
  • No stress for parents or grandparents

In just 30 minutes, you experience Tenerife’s most iconic and hidden landscapes, comfortably, indoors, and together.

Inside FlyX
The 5D experience

Not just a film. A flight you feel.

Feel the Wind

Gentle wind effects enhance the sensation of flight as you glide over cliffs and coastlines, adding realism without overwhelming the senses.

Flight Motion Seats

Smooth, controlled motion seats follow the flight path, creating the feeling of lift and movement while remaining comfortable for all ages.

Scents of Nature

Subtle natural scents accompany different landscapes, enhancing the feeling of forests, ocean air and volcanic environments without being overpowering.

Wraparound Screen

A large wraparound screen fills your field of vision, creating depth and scale that makes you feel fully surrounded by Tenerife’s landscapes.
