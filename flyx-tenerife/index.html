FAQ

Before you fly.

Still deciding? These are the questions guests ask most.

Will I get motion sick?

The motion is smooth and controlled, designed so the whole family can enjoy it. If you are sensitive, choose a seat and simply close your eyes during the steeper moments.

How long does it take?

About 30 minutes: a 15-minute film on the island's history, then a 15-minute flight on moving seats.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. We recommend FlyX from age 4. Babies under 1 year cannot ride.

Who should not ride?

For your safety, the flight is not recommended for:

  • Pregnant guests
  • Guests with heart conditions or epilepsy
  • Babies under 1 year
Is the footage real?

The flight uses real aerial footage filmed across Tenerife. A few scenes are AI-assisted, only where filming was not possible or to protect marine wildlife.

Do I need to book in advance?

Booking online secures your session time. Sessions leave every 30 minutes from 10:00 to 22:00.

Are there discounts?

Yes. Canary Islands residents, families and groups get special rates.

What if it rains?

FlyX is fully indoors, so every session runs in any weather.
