# FlyX Tenerife — scroll rebuild brief

Self-authored under the client's direction (no separate interview). Sources: the client's
current site export (`FlyX_Tenerife_-_5D_Flying_Cinema.pdf`), the proposed structure
(`Javasolt_weboldal-struktura.pdf`), supplied videos and logo, and the client's feedback:
"too different from my original design, too AI, not enough animations and scroll
elements, not a 10k luxury website feeling".

## Decisions
- **Look:** keep the original brand: white ground, teal and gold type, gold metallic logo,
  footage-led. Make it more luxurious through restraint, scale, timing and motion, not a new palette.
- **Structure:** the 9-section conversion order from the structure PDF, using the original site's copy.
- **Scenes, not one world:** each section behaves differently; no continuous camera chain.
- **Assets:** client footage (4 clips) and stills pulled from the site export. Nothing generated.

## Journey
1. Wonder: the ocean hero, "The Tenerife You've Never Seen"
2. Curiosity: the promise, word by word ("places no road can reach")
3. Escape: three landscapes travel sideways under the hand
4. Sensation: the 5D features, felt one at a time on a dark ground
5. Clarity: how the 30 minutes unfold, a clock that runs with the scroll
6. Belonging: who it's for
7. Relief: why it's easier than driving the island (the pains get struck out)
8. Commitment: tickets
9. Trust: 5.00 from 388 reviews, the location, the FAQ
10. Resolution: the ocean returns full-bleed with the booking action

## Feeling curve
- Hero: awe (footage, then the frame lifts away like leaving the ground)
- Discover: calm attention (words light up as they are read)
- Landscapes: freedom (lateral travel, the only sideways motion on the page). **Peak.**
- 5D: anticipation (dark room, one sensation at a time)
- How: reassurance (a clock reaching 30:00)
- Who / Why: warmth, then relief
- Tickets → FAQ: confidence
- Final: resolution (the hero's ocean returns and fills the screen)

**Peak:** "you scroll down and the whole island slides past sideways in real footage."
It's the site where you fly over Tenerife before you've even booked.

## Signature move
A gold flight path, taken from the swoosh in the FlyX logo, threads down the page through the
margins and swoops across between sections. Its head always sits just below the reader's eyeline.
